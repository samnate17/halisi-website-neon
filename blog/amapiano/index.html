The Halisi Weekly: Halloween Night 🎃

News, recaps, and good vibes from behind the decks.

Welcome to the very first post of The Halisi Weekly. I’m DJ Halisi, and this is where I’ll be sharing what’s going on in my world as a DJ: show recaps, music I’m loving, and moments from the booth. Some weeks it’ll be a full breakdown of a night, and other weeks it might be something different. Either way, thanks for being here from the start.

And what a night to kick things off with. Saturday, October 31 was Halloween, and the room came dressed for it. Maji Mazuri, the Afrobeats night series run by DJ Big Bear, handled the décor, and the red lighting alone set the tone before a single track dropped. Spooky, stylish, and exactly the right mood. Yared, the owner, also made his rounds giving out shots, which helped the energy along.

I opened the night with Private School Amapiano to ease everyone in, keeping it smooth and groovy while the room filled up. Later in my set I picked up the tempo and moved into upbeat tracks, and that’s when the night really took off. DJ Nate followed with Afrobeats, and DJ Fiorah came on third with more gqom and Afrotech, which got the whole club jumping. Big Bear brought it back around with more amapiano, all while dressed as a literal bear. Yes, a full bear behind the decks.

The costumes deserve their own mention, because people really showed up. There was a SWAT officer, a cow, a Pharaoh and Cleopatra, Spiderman, and plenty more. I went as Mikasa from Attack on Titan, and if you haven’t watched the show yet, I recommend it to everyone. Great characters, a story that pulls you in, and you’ll be hooked.

Question for you: What was the best costume you saw? Leave it in the comments and tag a friend who was there.

Before I wrap up, a bittersweet note. This was the last event Naco Taco will host, as they’re closing their doors. Thank you to Naco Taco, to Yared, to Maji Mazuri, and to all the DJs and guests who made it a proper send-off. We’ll miss the place.

📅 Next up: November 12th. Mark your calendar and bring your friends. I’ll be sharing how that one goes, so come back and tell me if you were there.

Until next time.

— DJ Halisi

About

Afrobeats, Amapiano & Afrohouse — built for the dancefloor

Halisi is a Kenyan-born DJ who has spent the last three years turning private gigs, clubs, and day parties into unforgettable nights. Blending Afrobeats energy, Amapiano's log-drum groove, and the deep pulse of Afrohouse, every set is read live and built for the room in front of it.

From intimate private bookings to club nights and day parties, Halisi brings the same energy to every set — building a growing catalogue of mixes and originals along the way.
